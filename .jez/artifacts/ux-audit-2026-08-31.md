## Findings

### H1 — Inbox count ignores Circlo but list/chip include it
- **Layer**: Feedback
- **Severity**: High
- **Surface**: `/circle/messages` · 390×844
- **Persona**: مینا opens پیام‌ها and sees «۰ گفتگو» while a thread sits under «همه ۱»
- **Reproduce**:
  1. Open `/circle/messages` with only Circlo present
  2. Read h1 subtitle vs filter chip vs list
- **Observed**: Subtitle `۰ گفتگو`; chip `همه ۱`; Circlo row visible
- **Expected**: Subtitle matches visible inbox rows (۱ گفتگو / or «۱ از سیرکل»)
- **Evidence**: snapshot 11:55:35; a11y tree e10/e11 vs e1/e4
- **Suspected location**: `app/messages/MessagesClassic.tsx:111-141` (`inboxPeers` filters Circlo; subtitle uses that count; rows still inject Circlo)
- **Smallest possible patch**: Include Circlo in subtitle when `showCirclo`, e.g. `inboxPeers.length + (showCirclo ? 1 : 0)`, or change empty copy to «فقط اعلان‌های سیرکل»

### H2 — Profile shows seeded deal/response stats with empty circle
- **Layer**: Architecture / Trust
- **Severity**: High
- **Surface**: `/circle/profile`
- **Persona**: مینا with ۰ نفر در حلقه still sees «۱۲ معامله · پاسخ‌گویی ۹۵٪»
- **Reproduce**: Open profile on cold/empty-circle account
- **Observed**: SocialCreditCard shows 12 deals / 95%
- **Expected**: Hide or show «هنوز معامله‌ای ثبت نشده» until real deals exist
- **Evidence**: `08-profile-fake-stats.png`
- **Suspected location**: `lib/social-credit.ts:109-110` (`person.deals`, `person.responseRate ?? 80`); `components/SocialCreditCard.tsx:30-38`; seed/user payload for `me`
- **Smallest possible patch**: If `forSelf && myCircleCount === 0 && deals === 0` (or deals from seed), render empty copy; stop shipping fake `deals`/`responseRate` on new users

### H3 — Pending invite without name/phone labeled «لینک»
- **Layer**: Interaction / Copy
- **Severity**: High
- **Surface**: `/circle/circle` pending list + sheet title
- **Persona**: She picked «دوست» then sees a row named «لینک»
- **Reproduce**: First-run invite, skip optional phone, open حلقه‌ی من
- **Observed**: Title `لینک`, sub `هنوز نپیوسته`
- **Expected**: `دوست` (relation) or «دعوت دوست» when no name/phone
- **Evidence**: circle snapshot 11:49; `inviteRowCopy` fallback
- **Suspected location**: `lib/invite.ts:245-248`
- **Smallest possible patch**: Fallback `relationLabels[invite.relationType]` instead of `"لینک"`

### H4 — Activation shows «قدم ۳ از ۳» while still waiting on first member

- **Severity**: High
- **Surface**: `/circle` ActivationPath after listing published with pending invite

- **Observed**: Header `قدم ۳ از ۳` / `۲ انجام‌شده`; middle step status waiting

- **Evidence**: `07-home-after-listing.png`
- **Suspected location**: `components/ActivationPath.tsx:112-114` (`doneCount + 1`); `lib/activation.ts:215-227` (listing `done` checked before member gate)
- **Smallest possible patch**: Display `indexOf(currentId)+1` when `currentId` set; and/or keep listing upcoming until `memberDone`
