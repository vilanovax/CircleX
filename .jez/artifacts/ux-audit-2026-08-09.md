## Findings

### H-axe — Endorsement + faint text fail WCAG contrast ✓ fixed
- **Status**: ✓ fixed (2026-08-09 re-verify — axe Serious 0 on `/circle`; colors `rgb(31,107,66)` / `rgb(111,106,100)`)
- **Layer**: Visual  
- **Severity**: High (axe Serious → hard gate)  
- **Surface**: `/circle` @ 390 / default panes  
- **Persona**: Mina skim-reads green trust lines; low contrast = unread  
- **Reproduce**:
  1. Open `/circle`
  2. Inject axe-core 4.10, `axe.run()`
  3. See `color-contrast` impact=serious, 11 nodes  
- **Observed**: `#2d8a56` on `#fffcf8` = 4.2:1; `#9a948e` on `#fffcf8` = 2.93:1  
- **Expected**: ≥4.5:1 for normal text  
- **Evidence**: CDP axe result 12:42; sample `a[href$=dariush] … text-levelA`  
- **Suspected location**: `tailwind.config.ts` (`levelA: "#2d8a56"`), `text-ink-faint` token; `components/TrustHighlight.tsx` endorsement line; `components/EventCard.tsx` meta  
- **Smallest possible patch**: Darken `levelA` to ~`#1f6b42` (or `#247a4a`) and `ink-faint` to ≥ `#6f6963`; re-run axe on `/circle`.

### H1 — Feed attributes connector relation to the seller ✓ fixed
- **Status**: ✓ fixed (re-verify: feed shows «حسین · همکار سارا», not «خواهر شما»)
- **Layer**: Interaction / Architecture  
- **Severity**: High  
- **Surface**: `/circle` feed cards compact TrustHighlight; also requests/events lists  
- **Persona**: Mina decides trust in <2s; «حسین · خواهر شما» is actively misleading (male name + sister)

## Hold this in your hands

If Circle were a physical object, it would be a warm, pocketable address book with a purple clasp — the trust path idea is the thing you’d show a friend. But the clasp stickers are wrong: calling حسین your sister, flashing a false unread count, and washing the green vouch lines until they’re hard to read means you’d put it back in the drawer after one anxious evening of arranging a babysitter. Fix the three High issues and the object suddenly feels like something Mina would actually keep in her bag.
