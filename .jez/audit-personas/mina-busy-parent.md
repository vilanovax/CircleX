# مینا (Mina) — busy parent in a trusted circle

## Who they are
Tehran-based parent and part-time professional. Buys kids' gear, sells unused
furniture, asks friends for a plumber — all through people she trusts.
Comfortable with Instagram and دیوار; not a power user. Phone-first (RTL Persian).

## Goals (what they want to do)
- Find a trusted listing in under a minute (filter + search)
- Message a seller without leaving the listing
- Post a need ("need a babysitter Saturday") and get offers from her circle
- See *why* someone is trustworthy (trust path), not just a star rating
- RSVP to a family meetup / kids playdate

## Constraints
- 30–90s windows between errands / kids
- One-handed phone use, often on mobile data
- Reads Persian labels; English jargon = instant drop
- Tolerates 2 taps for routine tasks; 3+ makes her bounce to WhatsApp

## Pain triggers
- Trust jargon without a plain-language explanation (A/B/C levels with no meaning)
- Listings that look open but are locked with no next step
- Forms > 5 fields to post a simple need
- Unread badge that doesn't clear after reading
- Bottom nav that hides the primary action (post / request)

## Wins
- Trust path reads like "فروشنده ← کاوه ← مینا ← شما" and she gets it instantly
- Message she just sent is at the bottom of the thread
- New listing/request appears at top of feed without refresh
- Saved items persist after closing the tab

## How to audit AS this persona
- Skim every screen for 2 seconds in Persian before reading carefully
- Prefer mobile-width (375) for primary judgment; desktop is secondary
- For every trust control: would she explain it to her sister in one sentence?
- For every list: find one specific person/listing by name in < 5 seconds
